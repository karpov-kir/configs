## What one question carries

One message per choice. Beyond what `~/.kk-flavor/standards/skill-protocol.md` → **Orchestrators — interactive first** asks of any question:

- **The choice in outcome terms** — what it decides for everything built after it, not which library you like.
- **Two or three real options**, each with its cost: the dependency, the toolchain, the operational burden, the thing it makes hard later.

Where a number decides it — a request rate, a data volume, a retention window — bring the number you measured or the one you assumed, and say which it was.
