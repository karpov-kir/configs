# The technical round

The whole delta for `~/.claude/skills/idsd-build/SKILL.md` → **Phase 1**'s second round.

## What belongs in the round

**A choice belongs here when reversing it is expensive** — `~/.kk-flavor/standards/skill-protocol.md` → **Orchestrators — interactive first** is that test. At intent scope the limb reaching furthest is what else consumes the choice: a language, a test runner or a deployment shape is inherited by every later intent. Everything else you settle yourself and record in the decision log (`~/.claude/skills/idsd-qualify/SKILL.md` → **The decision log**).

**Read what the repo already chose before you bring anything.** A project with a datastore has settled that question; what is live is whether this intent's outcome fits it. Where the code answers, say so and move on.

## What one question carries

One message per choice. Beyond what `~/.kk-flavor/standards/skill-protocol.md` → **Orchestrators — interactive first** asks of any question:

- **The choice in outcome terms** — what it decides for every later intent, not which library you like.
- **Two or three real options**, each with its cost: the dependency, the toolchain, the operational burden, the thing it makes hard later.

Where a number decides it — a request rate, a data volume, a retention window — bring the number you measured or the one you assumed, and say which it was.

## What closes it

**A stack choice lands in the project's own `CLAUDE.md`** — or in `.idsd/constraints.md` through `idsd-charter` where a command can fail on its breach — **and you write it rather than proposing it**. Phase 3 proposes, and `constraints.md` holds every move, because nobody was asked; this round asked, and their answer is what both were waiting for. One that shapes only this intent lands in its ICE constraint through `idsd-intent`. **The decision log is not one of those homes** — a choice a human made is not a choice a stage settled.

**Where your caller cannot answer, the build waits** (`~/.kk-flavor/standards/skill-protocol.md` → **Caller**).
