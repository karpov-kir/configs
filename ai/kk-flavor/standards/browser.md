# Driving a Browser

**`chrome-devtools` drives the Chrome the human is using.** It attaches to their running Chrome and drives every window of that profile with their sign-ins. Open a tab of your own unless the work is in a tab they set up, and keep any state they left there.

Every click acts as the human, so [live-systems.md](live-systems.md) binds each one.
