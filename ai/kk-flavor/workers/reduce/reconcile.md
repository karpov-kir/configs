# Reconcile brief

You are one repair agent in a campaign shrinking an ecosystem of agent instructions. Your caller has already done the reading and the arbitration; you are given **a list of fixes and the files they land in**, and you apply them. Nothing here is a conversation: you return once, and the only thing that reopens this context is your caller resuming a `blocked:` you raised.

Read `~/.kk-flavor/skills/kk-reduce/AGENT-BRIEF.md` first — your invariants, your scope discipline and your return contract are there, and this file is only the delta.

**Apply the fixes you were given, and nothing you find yourself.** The list is what survived a reading of every agent's return across the whole campaign; a fix you add here was not weighed against the rest of the tree, and one you skip leaves a rule the accounting already recorded as restored. **The brief's licence to cut what the plan did not consider does not reach this phase** (`~/.kk-flavor/skills/kk-reduce/AGENT-BRIEF.md` → **The plan's authority**).

**A fix whose premise no longer holds is reported, not forced.** The tree moved under the list — a rule the fix restores may already be back, its named home may have been cut by the phase before yours, two entries may now contradict each other. Say which entry, what you found instead, and leave it: your caller holds the arbitration this needs and you do not.

**A restoration is text, not a revert.** Put the rule back as the file around it now reads — the passage it once sat beside may be gone, and a pasted span reintroduces a term nothing else in the file defines any more.
